# A little light. A hungry ocean.

You are a glowing forager in a flooded maze, grazing points of plankton in a trench you cannot yet see. Follow the cool pocket of your own light, or send a cyan sonar crest around a corner to learn what lies ahead. Every bite makes you brighter and opens your window into the dark a little wider. Beyond that circle, even familiar ground disappears.

Something amber drifts in the next passage. It might be a meal worth chasing. It might be a Lanternjaw hiding its teeth beneath a jellyfish bell. The brighter you burn, the farther it can find you. A quick cloud of ink can break its sight, if you leave it in time.

The other hunters make the darkness feel different. A violet wave means the eyeless Gloamfin is listening; your own sonar can give it exactly the fix it needs. A warm glow warns of a Flarefish, whose sudden bloom lights the maze through solid rock. For one breathtaking moment you can see everything nearby — and it can see you.

Clean out a maze and descend. Your score and remaining lives go with you, while new hunters join the chase and your sonar reaches less of the trench. Corners become escape routes, remembered passages become lifelines, and the next small light is always a decision.
